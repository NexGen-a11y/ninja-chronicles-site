Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Ninja Chronicles Uploader ("the app") is used only by its owner to post videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

  • Data we use: the sign-in tokens that YouTube and TikTok issue when the owner authorizes the app, and basic account information (account name) returned by those services.
  • How we use it: only to upload the owner's videos and check their upload status.
  • Storage: tokens are stored encrypted as private secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and are never shared or sold.
  • Other people's data: the app does not collect any data from viewers or other users.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the Google account or TikTok app settings, after which the stored tokens stop working.
